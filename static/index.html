Inspect source metadata, confirm HLG/BT.2020 compatibility, then choose an HLG HDR or Rec.709 SDR LUT export profile.

Default: Neutral HLG BT.2020 (identity LUT). Choose HLG for LUTs authored for HLG-encoded BT.2020 RGB. Choose Rec.709 for standard SDR creative LUTs: PhotoLUT will tone-map the HLG source to SDR BT.709 before applying the LUT, and the export will be SDR, not HDR. Camera-Log LUTs are not supported. Download neutral HLG LUT.

100%

Both export profiles use 10-bit HEVC. HLG mode preserves HLG/BT.2020 HDR; Rec.709 mode creates tone-mapped SDR/BT.709. Dolby Vision sources are not accepted because their dynamic metadata cannot currently be preserved. HDR10/PQ inputs are inspected but export is limited to HLG sources.
